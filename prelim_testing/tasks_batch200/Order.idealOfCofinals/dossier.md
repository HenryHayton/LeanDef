## VTask.idealOfCofinals

### Object

`VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟` is a specific order-ideal in a preorder `P` constructed as a witness to the **Rasiowa–Sikorski lemma**: given a base element `p` and a countably-indexed family of cofinal subsets of `P`, the construction produces a single ideal that simultaneously contains `p` and meets (has nonempty intersection with) every member of the family. An *ideal* here means a nonempty, downward-closed, upward-directed lower set.

### Signature

<!-- PINNED-SIGNATURE:BEGIN -->
VTask.idealOfCofinals : {P : Type u_1} -> [Preorder P] -> (p : P) -> {ι : Type u_2} -> [Encodable ι] -> (𝒟 : ι → Order.Cofinal P) -> Order.Ideal P
<!-- PINNED-SIGNATURE:END -->


The implicit argument `P` is the underlying preorder type, equipped with the implicit `[Preorder P]` instance. The explicit argument `p : P` is the distinguished base element that the resulting ideal is required to contain. The implicit type `ι` is the index type for the family of cofinal sets, required to be countable via the `[Encodable ι]` instance. The explicit argument `𝒟 : ι → Order.Cofinal P` is the countably-indexed family of cofinal subsets of `P` that the resulting ideal must meet.

### Conventions

There are no junk-value or boundary conventions to declare: the construction is total and well-defined for any preorder `P`, any element `p : P`, and any encodable family `𝒟` of cofinal sets. No inputs are "degenerate" in a way that forces any special-cased output.

### Worked examples

- Claim: For the natural numbers with their usual order, a singleton cofinal family, and base element 0, the ideal `VTask.idealOfCofinals 0 𝒟` contains 0.

- Claim: For any preorder `P`, base element `p`, and encodable family `𝒟 : ι → Order.Cofinal P`, the element `p` belongs to `VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟` (this is the content of `mem_idealOfCofinals`).

- Claim: For any preorder `P`, base element `p`, encodable family `𝒟 : ι → Order.Cofinal P`, and index `i : ι`, there exists an element `x : P` such that `x ∈ 𝒟 i` and `x ∈ VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟` (this is the content of `cofinal_meets_idealOfCofinals`).

- Claim: The carrier of `VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟` is downward closed: if `y ≤ x` and `x ∈ VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟`, then `y ∈ VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟`.

- Claim: The ideal `VTask.idealOfCofinals p 𝒟` is directed: for any two elements `x, y` in the ideal, there exists an element `z` in the ideal with `x ≤ z` and `y ≤ z`.

### Boundaries

- If the index type `ι` is empty (no cofinal sets to meet), the ideal is still well-defined and contains `p`; the vacuous meeting condition is trivially satisfied.
- If `P` has a top element, the ideals produced may or may not contain it depending on whether the cofinal sets force it.
- The construction uses the countability of `ι` (via `Encodable`) in an essential way; the lemma is false for uncountable families in general.
- The preorder `P` need not be a lattice or even a directed set; directedness of the ideal is guaranteed by the construction itself, not assumed of `P`.

### Not to be confused with

- `Order.Cofinal`: a cofinal subset of a preorder, one of the *inputs* consumed here, not the ideal being produced.
- `Order.Ideal.ofCofinal` or ad-hoc principal ideals: a principal ideal generated by a single element, which need not meet any prescribed family of cofinal sets.
- The Rasiowa–Sikorski lemma for Boolean algebras specifically: this construction works for general preorders, not only Boolean algebras or forcing posets.
