## VTask.powerBasis

### Object

Given a nonzero polynomial `f` over a field `K`, `VTask.powerBasis hf` is the canonical power basis for the quotient ring `AdjoinRoot f` (i.e., `K[X]/(f)`) over `K`. Concretely, this basis consists of the elements `1, α, α², …, α^(d−1)`, where `α` denotes the image of `X` in `AdjoinRoot f` (the "root" of `f`) and `d = natDegree f` is the degree of `f`. This is a `PowerBasis` structure: it packages the generator `α`, the dimension `d`, and the proof that `{1, α, …, α^(d−1)}` is a `K`-basis of `AdjoinRoot f`.

### Signature

<!-- PINNED-SIGNATURE:BEGIN -->
VTask.powerBasis : {K : Type u_5} -> [Field K] -> {f : Polynomial K} -> (hf : f ≠ 0) -> PowerBasis K (AdjoinRoot f)
<!-- PINNED-SIGNATURE:END -->


The implicit argument `K` is the coefficient field; it must be equipped with a `Field` instance. The implicit argument `f` is the polynomial over `K` whose root is being adjoined. The explicit argument `hf` is a proof that `f` is nonzero, which is required to ensure the degree is well-defined and the listed powers form a genuine basis.

### Conventions

No junk-value or out-of-domain conventions apply to this definition: the only input is a proof that `f ≠ 0`, and the construction is total over all nonzero polynomials over a field.

### Worked examples

- Claim: For any nonzero polynomial `f` over a field `K`, the generator of `VTask.powerBasis hf` is `AdjoinRoot.root f`.

- Claim: For any nonzero polynomial `f` over a field `K`, the dimension of `VTask.powerBasis hf` equals `f.natDegree`.

- Claim: The `i`-th basis vector of `VTask.powerBasis hf` equals `(AdjoinRoot.root f) ^ (i : ℕ)` for each valid index `i`.

- Claim: When `f = X^2 + 1` over `ℚ`, `VTask.powerBasis hf` has dimension 2 and generator equal to the image of `X`, realising `ℚ(i) ≅ ℚ[X]/(X²+1)` with basis `{1, i}`.

### Boundaries

- If `f` has degree 0 (i.e., `f` is a nonzero constant), then `natDegree f = 0`, so the power basis has dimension 0, and `AdjoinRoot f` is isomorphic to the trivial ring `K/(1) = 0` (since a nonzero constant generates the unit ideal). The construction is still valid.
- If `f` has degree 1, the power basis has a single element `{1}`, reflecting the isomorphism `K[X]/(aX+b) ≅ K`.
- The construction does not require `f` to be irreducible; it produces a valid `PowerBasis` for any nonzero `f`, but the resulting quotient ring `AdjoinRoot f` is a field (equivalently, the basis spans a domain) only when `f` is irreducible.
- The hypothesis `f ≠ 0` is genuinely required: the degree of the zero polynomial is 0 by convention in Mathlib, but the zero polynomial generates the zero ideal, so `AdjoinRoot 0 ≅ K[X]`, which is infinite-dimensional over `K` and cannot have a finite power basis.

### Not to be confused with

- `AdjoinRoot.root f`: This is merely the generator element `α ∈ AdjoinRoot f`; `VTask.powerBasis` is the full `PowerBasis` structure packaging `α`, its powers, and their linear independence.
- `PowerBasis` (the structure type itself): `VTask.powerBasis hf` is a *term* of type `PowerBasis K (AdjoinRoot f)`, not the structure definition.
- `Algebra.adjoin`: This constructs a subalgebra generated by a set of elements, not a basis; do not confuse adjoining elements abstractly with the concrete quotient construction `AdjoinRoot f`.
