## Object

The *irrelevant ideal* of a graded ring `A = ⨁ᵢ 𝒜ᵢ` is the homogeneous ideal consisting of all elements whose degree-zero component is zero, i.e., the direct sum `⨁_{i > 0} 𝒜ᵢ`. In the classical case `ι = ℕ` this is the ideal of elements with vanishing constant term, and it plays the same role as the irrelevant ideal in classical projective algebraic geometry (the ideal generated by all homogeneous elements of positive degree). It is the kernel of the projection map `A → 𝒜₀`.

`VTask.irrelevant : {ι : Type u_1} -> {σ : Type u_2} -> {A : Type u_3} -> [Semiring A] -> [DecidableEq ι] -> [AddCommMonoid ι] -> [PartialOrder ι] -> [CanonicallyOrderedAdd ι] -> [SetLike σ A] -> [AddSubmonoidClass σ A] -> (𝒜 : ι → σ) -> [GradedRing 𝒜] -> HomogeneousIdeal 𝒜`

The index type `ι` is the grading monoid (typically `ℕ`). The type `σ` is the type of pieces (subgroups/submonoids of `A`). The type `A` is the ambient semiring. The argument `𝒜 : ι → σ` assigns to each grade index `i` the piece `𝒜 i` of the graded ring; together with the `GradedRing 𝒜` instance this expresses that `A` decomposes as an internal direct sum of the `𝒜 i`.

## Conventions

There are no junk-value or edge conventions to declare: the definition is total and well-defined for any graded ring satisfying the stated type-class hypotheses, including trivial gradings where all positive-degree pieces are zero (in which case the irrelevant ideal is the zero ideal).

## Worked examples

- Claim: An element `a : A` belongs to `VTask.irrelevant 𝒜` if and only if its degree-zero projection `proj 𝒜 0 a` is zero.

- Claim: Every homogeneous element of positive degree `i > 0` belongs to `VTask.irrelevant 𝒜`; that is, if `x ∈ 𝒜 i` and `0 < i`, then `x ∈ VTask.irrelevant 𝒜`.

- Claim: The underlying ideal of `VTask.irrelevant 𝒜` equals the span of the union `⋃ i > 0, 𝒜 i`.

- Claim: As an add-submonoid, `VTask.irrelevant 𝒜` equals the supremum `⨆ i > 0, AddSubmonoid.ofClass (𝒜 i)`.

## Boundaries

- When every piece `𝒜 i` for `i > 0` is the zero subgroup (a trivially graded ring), the irrelevant ideal is the zero ideal `{0}`.
- The irrelevant ideal is never the entire ring (assuming the ring is nontrivial and the unit `1` lives in `𝒜₀`, which is guaranteed by the `GradedRing` axioms): the element `1` has nonzero degree-zero component, so `1 ∉ VTask.irrelevant 𝒜`.
- The condition `CanonicallyOrderedAdd ι` ensures `0` is the bottom of the ordering; the notion of "positive degree" (`i > 0`) is therefore well-formed and the irrelevant ideal is the complement of the degree-zero piece.
- The ideal is properly contained in `A` precisely when the ring is nontrivial.

## Not to be confused with

- **The zero ideal** `⊥ : HomogeneousIdeal 𝒜` — the irrelevant ideal contains all positive-degree homogeneous elements and is typically strictly larger than `{0}`.
- **The augmentation ideal** in group-ring theory — while superficially similar (kernel of a ring map), the augmentation ideal lives in a group ring, not a graded ring, and uses a different projection.
- **`HomogeneousIdeal.top`** (the whole ring as a homogeneous ideal) — the irrelevant ideal is generally a proper ideal, not the unit ideal.
