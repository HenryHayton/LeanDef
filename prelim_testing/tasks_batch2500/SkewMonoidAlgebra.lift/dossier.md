## Object

`VTask.lift` is the canonical equivalence (a bijection that is also structure-preserving in both directions) between

- monoid homomorphisms from a monoid `G` to a `k`-algebra `A`, and  
- `k`-algebra homomorphisms from the skew monoid algebra `SkewMonoidAlgebra k G` to `A`.

Informally, it says that a monoid map `G →* A` determines, and is uniquely determined by, a `k`-algebra map out of `SkewMonoidAlgebra k G`.  This is the universal property of the skew monoid algebra: it is the "freest" `k`-algebra generated by `G` subject to the twisted multiplication coming from the `G`-action on `k`.

`VTask.lift : (k : Type u_1) -> (G : Type u_2) -> [CommSemiring k] -> [Monoid G] -> (A : Type u_4) -> [Semiring A] -> [Algebra k A] -> [MulSemiringAction G k] -> [SMulCommClass G k k] -> (G →* A) ≃ (SkewMonoidAlgebra k G →ₐ[k] A)`

- `k` is the commutative semiring of scalars; it serves as the ground ring over which the skew monoid algebra is formed and over which all algebra maps are `k`-linear.
- `G` is the monoid whose elements index the basis of the skew monoid algebra; the `G`-action on `k` twists the multiplication.
- `A` is the target `k`-algebra; it is the codomain of both the monoid maps and the algebra maps being compared.
- The instance `[MulSemiringAction G k]` records the twisted action of `G` on `k` that defines the skew multiplication.
- The instance `[SMulCommClass G k k]` ensures that the `G`-action on `k` commutes with scalar multiplication by `k`, a compatibility needed for the universal property to hold.

## Conventions

No special junk-value or boundary conventions are declared for this definition: it is a total equivalence between well-typed sets of structure-preserving maps, and every input satisfying the stated type-class hypotheses yields a well-defined equivalence.

## Worked examples

- Claim: For the trivial monoid `Unit` with trivial action on `k`, `VTask.lift k Unit` sends the unique monoid map `Unit →* A` (the one sending `()` to `1`) to the unique `k`-algebra map `SkewMonoidAlgebra k Unit →ₐ[k] A` determined by that generator image.

- Claim: If `φ : G →* A` is a monoid homomorphism, then applying `(VTask.lift k G A).toFun φ` to the canonical generator `of k G g` in `SkewMonoidAlgebra k G` yields the same element as `φ g`; in other words, the algebra map extends `φ` along the canonical inclusion `G → SkewMonoidAlgebra k G`.

- Claim: The two directions of the equivalence are inverse to each other: for any `φ : G →* A`, composing the lifted algebra map with the canonical monoid inclusion `of k G` recovers `φ` exactly; and for any algebra map `f : SkewMonoidAlgebra k G →ₐ[k] A`, lifting the restricted monoid map `f ∘ of k G` yields back `f`.

## Boundaries

- When `G` is the trivial monoid `{1}`, the skew monoid algebra is isomorphic to `k` itself, and the equivalence reduces to the identification of `k`-algebra maps `k →ₐ[k] A` with the unique point (the structure map).
- When the action of `G` on `k` is trivial (every `g • r = r`), the skew monoid algebra coincides with the ordinary monoid algebra `MonoidAlgebra k G`, and `VTask.lift` specialises to the universal property of the ordinary monoid algebra.
- The equivalence is defined for semirings (not just rings), so it applies in the non-negative or characteristic-`p` settings without any extra hypotheses.
- No element of `SkewMonoidAlgebra k G` is outside the domain of the lifted algebra map; the map is defined on the entire algebra.

## Not to be confused with

- `MonoidAlgebra.lift`: the analogous universal-property equivalence for the *untwisted* monoid algebra, where the `G`-action on `k` is assumed trivial.
- `VTask.of`: the canonical monoid map `G →* SkewMonoidAlgebra k G` that embeds generators; this is the map that `VTask.lift` inverts (in the sense that the inverse of the equivalence composes a `k`-algebra map with `of`).
- `MulSemiringAction.toAlgHom`: a construction that turns a ring action into an algebra map, which is related but concerns automorphisms of the algebra rather than the universal property of a free object.
