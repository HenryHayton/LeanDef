## Object

The derived series of a group is the canonical descending chain of subgroups obtained by starting with the whole group and repeatedly replacing each term by its commutator subgroup (the subgroup generated by all commutators of pairs of elements from the previous term). The $n$-th term of the derived series is often written $G^{(n)}$ or $D^n(G)$; it measures how far the group is from being abelian in a cumulative, iterated sense.

`VTask.derivedSeries : (G : Type u_1) -> [Group G] -> ℕ → Subgroup G`

The first argument `G` is the ambient group (a type equipped with a group structure via the instance `[Group G]`). The natural number argument `n` is the index into the series, indicating how many times the commutator-subgroup operation has been applied starting from the full group.

## Conventions

At index 0 the result is the top (full) subgroup ⊤ of G, i.e., $G^{(0)} = G$ itself. For each successive index the result is the commutator subgroup of the previous term with itself, so no special junk value or out-of-domain convention is needed; the function is total on all natural numbers.

## Worked examples

- Claim: `VTask.derivedSeries G 0 = ⊤` for any group `G`.
  ```lean
  example (G : Type*) [Group G] : VTask.derivedSeries G 0 = ⊤ := rfl
  ```

- Claim: `VTask.derivedSeries G 1` equals the commutator subgroup `⁅(⊤ : Subgroup G), ⊤⁆` (the derived subgroup of G).
  ```lean
  example (G : Type*) [Group G] : VTask.derivedSeries G 1 = ⁅(⊤ : Subgroup G), ⊤⁆ := rfl
  ```

- Claim: `VTask.derivedSeries G (n + 1) = ⁅VTask.derivedSeries G n, VTask.derivedSeries G n⁆` for any `n`.
  ```lean
  example (G : Type*) [Group G] (n : ℕ) :
      VTask.derivedSeries G (n + 1) = ⁅VTask.derivedSeries G n, VTask.derivedSeries G n⁆ := rfl
  ```

- Claim: For an abelian group `A`, every term `VTask.derivedSeries A n` with `n ≥ 1` is contained in the trivial subgroup, because all commutators vanish.

## Boundaries

- At `n = 0`: the result is always the entire group `⊤`, regardless of whether `G` is abelian or not.
- For an abelian group: `VTask.derivedSeries G 1 = ⊥` (the trivial subgroup), and all higher terms are also trivial.
- For a non-abelian simple group (such as $A_5$): every term of the derived series equals the full group, since the group equals its own commutator subgroup.
- The series is descending: each term is a normal subgroup of the preceding term, and each term is contained in all previous ones.
- A group `G` is solvable if and only if `VTask.derivedSeries G n = ⊥` for some `n : ℕ`.

## Not to be confused with

- The **lower central series** `lowerCentralSeries G n`: that series takes commutators of successive terms with the full group, not with themselves, and measures nilpotency rather than solvability.
- The **upper central series** `upperCentralSeries G n`: an ascending series built from centres, also related to nilpotency rather than solvability.
- The **commutator subgroup** `commutator G` (also written `⁅G, G⁆`): this is just `VTask.derivedSeries G 1`, the single first step of the derived series, not the full iterated construction.
